# Workshops

Hands-on workshops for people moving into AI safety, run with AI Safety Berlin. Each workshop has its own folder with a printable handout and copy-ready AI prompts, so participants can keep working after the session.

## Workshops

| Workshop | Date | What you leave with | Materials |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your 30-Second AI Safety Intro | 12 Nov 2026 | A spoken intro (one-to-one and group versions), a LinkedIn headline and About section, a Swapcard profile, and outreach and follow-up messages | [Prompt sheet](https://aaronmpinto.github.io/workshops/intro/) · [Handout (PDF)](https://aaronmpinto.github.io/workshops/intro/handout.pdf) |

## Using the prompts

1. Open any AI chat, such as Claude or ChatGPT.
2. Paste the **Start here** prompt with your facts filled in.
3. Paste the prompt for the step you're working on.

Every prompt tells the AI to use only the facts you give it and to add nothing. Read the result out loud: if it doesn't sound like you, keep your own version.
